HI!I’m Chen Sicheng , an undergraduate in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Tsinghua University , major in Intelligent Manufacturing and Equipment with a focus on robotics. My research interests include embodied AI, robotic manipulation, coordinated dual-arm control, motion planning and optimal control, and robot perception.

## Research and projects

### Dual-arm teleoperation and collision avoidance

**Tsinghua undergraduate research program · Since April 2026**

I contribute to a Franka Panda system for dual-arm teleoperation and collision avoidance, with experiments in simulation and on hardware. I built the wrist camera and 3D perception pipeline and am independently developing a model predictive controller (MPC) using cuRobo v2.
